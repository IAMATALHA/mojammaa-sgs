Privacy Policy

Application: Mojammaa Connect (Android package and Apple bundle identifier com.youssefatalha.mojammaasgs)
Last updated: 2026-08-30

This Privacy Policy describes how the Mojammaa Connect mobile application (the “App”) collects, uses, and protects information when used by adult parents or legal guardians and authorised school staff. The App is not intended for use by pupils. It is operated by the school administration of Mojammaa and is hosted on Google Firebase (project mojammaa-sgs).

1. Who is the data controller

The school administration that deploys the App for its pupils is the data controller. Technical operation of the backend (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Firebase Cloud Messaging) is provided by Google LLC as a sub-processor.

2. What information we collect

  • Account and profile information — user identifier, email address, optional contact telephone number, first and last name, role and school, route or vehicle assignments. Passwords are submitted directly to Firebase Authentication; the App operator does not receive or store them in plain text.
  • School records — for pupils enrolled at the school: full name, official school identifier (including the MASSAR code), date of birth, class/level, parent or guardian account link, grades (notes), attendance records, homework (devoirs) and teacher feedback. This data is entered by teachers and administrators, not by the pupils themselves.
  • School operations — schedules and school-calendar entries; behaviour records; parent absence declarations and their reason (which may contain health information); transport route, stop and vehicle assignments; pickup requests and statuses; and collective class prayer status. Prayer status is recorded for the class as a whole and does not contain an individual pupil attendance list.
  • Messages and activity — school announcements, direct messages between authorised users, message attachments, read receipts, homework submissions and related timestamps.
  • Uploaded files — documents and images that an authorised parent, teacher or administrator chooses to upload (for example, homework evidence, course materials or class photos), stored in Firebase Cloud Storage. Camera, photo-library and file access occurs only after the user initiates the corresponding action.
  • Push notification identifiers — when notifications are enabled, the App registers an Expo push token so the school can send reminders (new grade posted, announcement, etc.). Expo routes delivery through Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) on Android and Apple Push Notification service (APNs) on iOS; those services process their platform-specific installation or device identifiers. The Expo push token is stored against the user’s account in Firestore.
  • Security and login records — automatically when an authenticated session starts, we record the account identifier, email, role, login time, IP address and a limited forwarded-IP chain when supplied by the network infrastructure. We also record the platform, operating-system version, App version and, where the operating system exposes them, device brand, manufacturer, model or device class. These records are used only for account security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.
  • Language preference and theme — stored locally on the device (AsyncStorage) for in-app personalisation only.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, contacts, microphone recordings or browsing history. The App does not request Android precise or approximate location permissions and does not access live device location. It stores only school-provided transport route or stop labels as described above, and does not derive or store a city, country or GPS location from the security IP address. The App contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party artificial-intelligence integration.

3. How we use the information

  • To authenticate adult users and enforce role-based access (admin, teacher, parent/legal guardian and authorised driver).
  • To display class schedules, grades, attendance, homework and messages to the people authorised to see them under the school’s Firestore security rules.
  • To deliver push notifications related to school activity.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the service, including login auditing, abuse prevention, troubleshooting and backups.
  • To send a password-reset email when a user explicitly requests one.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Legal basis

Processing is carried out (a) to perform the educational contract between the school and the pupil’s family, (b) on the basis of the school’s legitimate interest in running its educational activities, and (c) where applicable, with the consent obtained from the parent or legal guardian at enrolment.

5. Children’s data

The App is intended for adults: parents or legal guardians and authorised school staff. It is not directed to children, and pupils do not create or use App accounts. The school nevertheless processes educational records about pupils, including pupils under the age of 13, so that authorised adults can manage and follow their education.

  • We collect only the information strictly necessary for the school’s educational activity.
  • We do not show advertising and we do not engage in behavioural profiling of minors.
  • A parent or legal guardian may, at any time, request access to, correction of, or deletion of their child’s data by contacting the school administration (see Section 9).
  • The App contains no anonymous or random chat. Messages are restricted to authenticated school accounts, identify the sending account and are limited to authorised parents, teachers and school administrators.

6. Sharing and sub-processors

Personal data is hosted on Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) and may be transferred and processed in data centres operated by Google. We share data only with:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — Authentication, Cloud Functions, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging and hosting.
  • Expo (Expo Application Services, 650 Industries, Inc.) — push-notification routing and delivery of signed App updates.
  • Apple Push Notification service (Apple Inc.) — push-notification delivery on iOS.
  • Resend — the user’s email address and password-reset email are processed only when that user requests a password reset.

These providers process data on behalf of the App operator to deliver the functions described above. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We may disclose information where required by law.

7. Data retention

  • Security and login records are scheduled for automatic deletion after 180 days.
  • School records, operational records and school messages are retained while the pupil is enrolled and for any additional period required by applicable education or record-keeping rules.
  • The Firebase Authentication account and main user profile are deleted when the account is removed. Related school and operational records follow the retention rules above or any legal retention requirement.
  • Uploaded files are retained until deleted by an authorised user or as part of the related school record.
  • After deletion from the live database, Firestore records may remain in encrypted backups for up to seven days before those backups expire.
  • Local preferences remain on the device until App data is cleared or the App is uninstalled.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Data at rest is encrypted by Google Cloud. Access to data is enforced by Firestore and Cloud Storage security rules, which restrict each user (admin / teacher / parent / driver) to the records they are authorised to see. Passwords are stored only as hashes by Firebase Authentication.

9. Your rights & contact

Users (or, for minors, their parents or legal guardians) may request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of personal data, and may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Accounts are created by the school and cannot be self-registered. To request account and associated data deletion, send an email identifying the account to the contact below. The school may verify the requester’s identity before acting and will explain any record that must be retained under applicable law. The same contact can be used for any privacy question:

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates when the latest changes took effect. Material changes will be communicated through the App or by the school administration.

This policy applies to the Mojammaa Connect mobile application distributed on Google Play and the Apple App Store.
